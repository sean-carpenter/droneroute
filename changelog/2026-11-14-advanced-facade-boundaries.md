## Summary

Facade scans can follow a circle, a spiral, or a building polygon, not only a single wall. The drone stays a chosen distance outside the structure.

## Changes

- Circle and spiral: drag from the center to the outer wall, then pick level orbits, vertical columns, or one climbing spiral
- A second radius tapers orbits and the spiral. Vertical columns stay on the bottom radius
- Polygon: click the corners of a building. The path is that outline pushed outward by the target distance
- Photo spacing comes from camera overlap at the target distance, or from a horizontal and vertical distance you type
- The panel shows how many orbits, columns, or turns that produces, and warns if the path crosses itself or the photo count is capped
