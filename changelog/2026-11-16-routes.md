# Routes: one group of waypoints per template

The waypoint list was one long list however many templates were applied. Now
each template becomes a route of its own, named after it, and hand-placed
waypoints go into the selected route ("New route" starts one). Routes can be
renamed, folded, selected as a whole, moved earlier or later in the flight,
and deleted with their waypoints. The mission still flies and exports as a
single path, route after route. Routes are saved with the mission (a new
`routes` column; each waypoint records its `routeId`), and older missions open
with everything in "Route 1". Automatic waypoint names now follow their
position.
