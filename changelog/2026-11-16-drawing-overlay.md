# Lay a PDF drawing over the map

Drop a one-page PDF, such as a permit set's site plan, on the editor (or use
**Add drawing** in the Site section) and it appears over the satellite view
with the paper see-through. Drag the middle to move it, a corner to resize it
and the round handle to rotate it until it lines up, then trace the buildings
as site features. Drawings are saved with the mission and copied when it is
duplicated, shared or cloned. The PDF is drawn in the browser with pdf.js; only
the resulting image is stored. Multi-page PDFs are refused for now.
