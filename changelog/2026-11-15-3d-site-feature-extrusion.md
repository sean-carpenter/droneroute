# Site features and obstacles rise to their height in 3D

In 3D view, a building drawn as a site feature now rises to its height as a
solid block in its own color, and an obstacle with a known height rises as a
translucent red block — the same way the map's own buildings are extruded.
Lots and shapes without a height stay flat. Clicking an extruded block selects
the feature, just like clicking its footprint.

This makes it possible to judge a route against the site by eye: a facade
inspection path can be checked against the wall it inspects, and a climb can
be checked against the shed it passes over.
