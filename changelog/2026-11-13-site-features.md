## Summary

Draw a site's buildings and areas on the map as colored, named, editable
shapes that are saved with the mission.

## Changes

- New "Site" section in the sidebar: draw an existing building, garage,
  addition, lot or other area; each kind has its own color and name
- Click a building on the map and choose "Add as building" to add its outline
- Select a feature in the list or on the map; drag its name to move it, drag,
  add or remove corners, and change its name, kind, color, height and notes
- Buildings count as obstacles in the route checks by default; a lot does not
- Saved with the mission, copied when sharing or cloning, and covered by undo
