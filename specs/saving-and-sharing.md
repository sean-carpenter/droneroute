# Saving and sharing

Save your missions to your account and share them with others via a link.

## What you can do

- **Save** a mission so you can come back to it later.
- **See all your saved missions** in one list. When you can save (signed in, or on a single-user instance), this list is the first thing the app shows.
- **Open** a saved mission from the list to edit it. The waypoint, point of interest, obstacle and mission settings panels belong to the editor, so they appear once a mission is open.
- **Start a new mission** from the list.
- **Delete** missions you no longer need.
- **Share** a mission by generating a read-only link that anyone can open — no account required.
- **Revoke** a shared link at any time, immediately making it inaccessible.
- Visitors who open a shared link can:
  - View the mission details (drone, waypoint count, distance, altitude, estimated time).
  - Open the mission in the editor.
  - Clone the mission to their own account.
  - Export the mission as a KMZ file.

## How it works

1. The app opens on the saved missions list. Click a mission to open it in the editor, or click **New mission**.
2. In the editor, type a name in the box at the top and click **Save**. A message confirms the name it was saved under.
3. Click the folder icon in the editor to go back to the list. The mission that is open in the editor is marked there.
4. To share, hover a mission in the list and click the share button. Copy the generated link and send it to anyone.

## Good to know

- Opening another mission or starting a new one while the editor has unsaved changes asks before discarding them.
- Guests (no account, not single-user) have nothing saved, so the app opens straight in the editor for them.
- You need an account to save missions. Without one, you can still plan flights but they won't persist. The exception is a self-hosted instance in single-user mode, where everything saves to the local workspace with no sign-in.
- Shared links are read-only — viewers cannot modify your original mission.
- Sharing is available in cloud mode. Self-hosted installations may not include this feature.
